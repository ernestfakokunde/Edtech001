# RecappEdu

RecappEdu is a study companion for university students. It helps students turn
their own learning materials into focused practice and keep their study work
organized.

Students can create flashcards or timed quizzes from uploaded documents, save
quizzes with their answers and explanations, and revisit past results. Courses
can be added for organization, or skipped for a quicker start. A shared paper
repository helps students discover and contribute course materials.

The app also includes study rewards, referrals, and tools for reviewing shared
materials. Its goal is to make it easier to prepare, practise, and keep track of
what to study next.
